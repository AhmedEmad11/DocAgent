Pricing and Plans, effective January 2026.

The Basic plan costs 12 dollars per month and includes 5 users. The Pro plan costs 25 dollars per month and includes 40 users. The new Team plan costs 18 dollars per month and includes 15 users. The Enterprise plan is priced individually after a sales call.

Support. Basic plan customers receive email support with a reply within 48 hours. Pro plan customers receive email and chat support with a reply within 4 hours.

Billing. Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within 14 days. Annual billing is available with a discount of two months.

Storage. The Basic plan includes 20 GB of storage per account and the Pro plan includes 200 GB.
