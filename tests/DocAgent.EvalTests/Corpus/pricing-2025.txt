Pricing and Plans, effective January 2025.

The Basic plan costs 10 dollars per month and includes 5 users. The Pro plan costs 20 dollars per month and includes 25 users. The Enterprise plan is priced individually after a sales call.

Support. Basic plan customers receive email support with a reply within 48 hours. Pro plan customers receive email support with a reply within 24 hours.

Billing. Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within 30 days. Annual billing is available with a discount of one month.

Storage. The Basic plan includes 10 GB of storage per account and the Pro plan includes 100 GB.
